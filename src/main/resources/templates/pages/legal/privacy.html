Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains how Weresona (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects personal data when you use Resona AI (the “Service”), in line with Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

1. Contact and Data Protection Officer

For any privacy question or request, contact our Data Protection Officer at numi@weresona.com.

2. What we collect

DataExamples
Account detailsEmail address, name and job role (if you add them), password (stored only in encrypted, hashed form)

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

3. How we use it

  • to provide the Service: accounts, stores, music, jingles and the store player;
  • to send account emails, such as email confirmation, password resets and store access details;
  • to turn your announcement text into voice audio;
  • to show you usage statistics for your stores;
  • to keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems;
  • to respond to your questions and requests; and
  • to meet legal obligations.

By using the Service you consent to this use. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us, but we may then be unable to provide some or all of the Service.

5. Transfers outside Singapore

Some providers process data outside Singapore. We rely on their contractual commitments to protect personal data to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

6. How long we keep it

We keep personal data while your account is active. After an account is closed, we delete or anonymise its personal data within 90 days, unless we must keep it longer by law. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

7. How we protect it

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as secure hashes, access to systems is restricted, and backups are kept. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPA you can:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you and how it has been used;
  • ask us to correct it; and
  • withdraw your consent.

Email numi@weresona.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

9. Who the Service is for

The Service is for businesses and isn’t intended for anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here and, for important changes, email you.
